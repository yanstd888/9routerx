# Udio music generation native lane
Add the isolated `udio` provider from OmniRoute pin `632d84f569f72893ed148e5b19dd05e2fff1a52a`: a session-cookie music lane on the pinned `https://www.udio.com/api/generate-proxy` POST with `{prompt, samplerOptions:{seed:-1}}`, followed by bounded `/api/songs` polling for finished `song_path` values and a guarded audio download. Polling stops after 300 seconds, audio downloads cap at 50 MiB with at most three redirects, and every hop passes the router's SSRF guard with loopback only under the existing operator switches. The reply is folded into the router's OpenAI music shape `{created, data:[{b64_json, format:"mp3"}]}`. Artwork is rasterized from the genuine pinned `udio.svg`. Verification: offline helper tests pin default/status/audio URL derivation, cookie resolution, guarded download and metadata-block refusal, plus frontend registry/model/artwork assertions. No live provider, login or quota validation was performed.
